What is Esports?

Short for electronic sports, esports are a subset of gaming video content and are defined by EMARKETER as organized gaming competitions among professional players and teams. Like traditional sports, many esports tend to feature the same structure, such as having leagues, teams, and players.

The advantages you get from using these equipment.



Gaming Headset

  • Immersive audio
Gaming headsets can feature spatial audio or virtual surround sound technology to enhance the gaming experience.
  • Noise isolation
Gaming headsets are designed to block out external distractions and ambient noise so you can focus on the game.
  • Communication
Many gaming headsets have built-in microphones that allow you to communicate with teammates during online gameplay. The microphones are designed to isolate background noise and pick up your voice more clearly.
  • Durability
Gaming headsets are built to withstand the demands of gaming, which can involve intense use and rough handling. They are often made with sturdy materials and stronger cables than regular headphones.

Modded Controller

  • Rapid Fire Mode
One of the most common features of modded controllers is rapid fire. This allows players to automatically fire weapons at a faster rate than normally possible by simply holding down a button. This feature is particularly popular in first-person shooters (FPS) where rapid firing can provide a significant advantage in combat situations.
  • Button Remapping
Modded controllers often allow for custom button remapping, meaning you can change the function of certain buttons to better suit your playstyle. For example, you can map jump or crouch actions to buttons that are easier for you to reach, making your gameplay more fluid and intuitive.
  • Quick scoping
In FPS games, quick scoping is a technique where players quickly zoom in with a sniper rifle and shoot in a swift motion. Modded controllers can have a quick scope feature that automates this process, allowing for faster and more accurate shots.

Gaming Computer

  • Upgradability
Gaming desktops are usually more upgradable than gaming laptops, allowing you to customize and enhance your system over time.
  • Performance
Gaming desktops are designed to handle the demands of modern games, offering higher frame rates, smoother gameplay, and superior graphics.
  • Cooling
Gaming desktops often have advanced cooling systems to prevent overheating during long gaming sessions. The larger chassis of a desktop also allows for more fans, which improves cooling and heat dissipation.
  • Customization
You can customize your gaming desktop with additional features like RGB lighting and customizable components.Compatibility
