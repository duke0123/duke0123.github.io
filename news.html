Dr Disrespect

In 2019, Dr. Disrespect, whose real name is Guy Beahm, was banned from Twitch and E3 after he livestreamed himself entering a public bathroom at the Los Angeles Convention Center during E3

why was he banned?

  • Beahm's actions violated Twitch's community guidelines and California's invasion of privacy laws: Community guidelines: Beahm's actions violated the guideline that states streamers must respect local, national, and international laws.
  • Invasion of privacy laws: California's penal code 647 section J states that people have a reasonable expectation of privacy in public bathrooms, and it's a crime to use a camera in a bathroom in most circumstances.

XXif

Damion "XXiF" Cook, a professional Fortnite player, was disqualified from the 2019 Fortnite World Cup Finals after being found to have cheated.

What happened?

  • In April 2019, Epic Games discovered that Cook and other players colluded during a Week 3 Fortnite World Cup Online Open competition. Cook and the other players were ineligible for the week's prizes and were given a 14-day competitive ban. What are the consequences of cheating in Fortnite?
  • Players who violate Fortnite's Community Rules or tournament rules may be temporarily or permanently banned from the game. Temporary bans can last up to 30 days, while permanent bans are indefinite.
